# Written text and character context

Follow `../../references/written-text-and-character-response.md` and `../dialogue-selection/SKILL.md`. Load approved names and scoped project decisions before drafting. Share the existing event ledger with character-voice review and reuse completed checks; cross-references do not require recursive rerouting of unchanged events.

1. Inspect the actual writing and surrounding scene with available media tools. OCR and source subtitles are aids; retain unreadable or uncertain text for review rather than inventing it. Do not claim an OCR/playback capability is connected merely because this workflow calls for one.
2. Record readable visual bounds and meaningful stages, exact supported wording, selection decision/reason, linked speech/reaction IDs and evidence for character knowledge. Distinguish audience visibility from character comprehension.
3. Compare writing with spoken reading or interpretation. Preserve exact reading, partial reading, paraphrase, meaningful misreading, deception and reaction as distinct events; keep the text author's style distinct from the speaking character's voice.
4. Translate only information disclosed at that stage. Preserve unfinished or deleted drafts, references and deliberate uncertainty. Select any extra text cue by its information value and timing, checking differences that speech alone does not transfer.
5. Author visual timing, combined reading load and presentation under `../../references/subtitle-editorial-rules.md`. Speech alignment and reaction timestamps do not substitute for readable visual boundaries.
6. Review the linked text–speech–reaction group and its reveal/payoff after each change. Log wording, interpretation, disclosure, duplication, timing and reading-load findings with evidence and status. Send material role/register questions to `../character-voice-review/SKILL.md` and obtain independent contextual review when available.

Keep essential unresolved events in REVIEW-IN-CONTEXT and report unavailable evidence honestly. Pass the exact output through `../full-watch-qc/SKILL.md` and `../../references/quality-gates.md`; frames or transcript review alone cannot establish continuous-watch PASS.

## Contextual language in writing

Apply `../../references/slang-and-free-translation.md` to the text author's intent before choosing Hebrew for a card, message, sign or document. In an evidenced flirtatious card written by a girl, "Do you like me?" can be "אני מוצאת חן בעיניך?"; in the user-approved Gravity Falls example this is the exact wording, including אני. Ordinary friendship, a different writer or addressee, and a literal quotation require their own contextual decision. Check the translated question, its choices and the character's reaction together, preserving reveal stages and distinctions between writing, reading and interpretation. A wording correction alone does not shift visual clocks.
