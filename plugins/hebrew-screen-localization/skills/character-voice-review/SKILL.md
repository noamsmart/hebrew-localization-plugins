# Character voice review

Read `../../references/character-voice-and-address.md` and load the continuity bible, scoped user decisions and approved names. Do not replace existing preferences with a new default. Share the existing event ledger with written-text review and reuse completed checks; cross-references do not require recursive rerouting of unchanged events.

1. Establish source access and available evidence. Build or reuse character, relationship, scene and cue records with speaker/addressee/reference IDs and evidence needed for linguistic decisions. Preserve intentional audience ambiguity.
2. Review complete exchanges against the source, checking who speaks, who is addressed, quoted perspective, gender/number agreement, address terms, communicative act and local register. Keep each character's voice distinct while preserving supported register changes.
3. Recheck condensed/adapted lines for factual and pragmatic fidelity under `../../references/quality-gates.md`. Flag a performed claim, lie or misunderstanding where a reviewer might otherwise replace it with later-known facts.
4. Inspect material speaker/agreement uncertainty through the available audiovisual route. A still image or diarization guess cannot establish speech attribution alone. Use a faithful neutral form where appropriate or retain a contextual-review item.
5. Send linked written-text readings and reactions through `../written-text-context/SKILL.md`. Check subtitle turn markers and presentation through `../../references/subtitle-editorial-rules.md`.
6. Log defects by cue/timecode with evidence, proposed correction and status. Recheck related turns after changes; obtain an independent review of material changes when available and record its actual coverage. Identify unavailable review rather than claiming it was done.

Report source-fidelity, voice, agreement and disclosure findings separately. Text review is not full-watch QC, and this skill adds no speaker-identification service by itself.
